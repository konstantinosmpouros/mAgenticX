# Research

When performing research tasks:

1. **Delegate first** — always send research queries to the `research` sub-agent rather than answering from memory alone.
2. **Scope the query** — provide the researcher with a precise, focused question. Avoid vague or overly broad queries.
3. **Validate results** — if the findings seem incomplete, run a follow-up query with more specific terms.
4. **Preserve sources** — include any URLs or references returned by the researcher in your final saved document.
